# Demo video

The included dist/demo.mp4 is an 84-second captioned screenshot walkthrough assembled from actual application UI states observed during verification. It has no voiceover and is not a continuous screen recording. It shows input/configuration, Instagram output, YouTube output, cancellation and architecture/limitations. The captions disclose simulated AI and local persistence.

For a stronger candidate-led demonstration, use DEMO_SCRIPT.md to record your own continuous walkthrough and explain the implementation in your own words. The assessment does not explicitly require a continuous recording, but candidate narration makes ownership easier to assess.
